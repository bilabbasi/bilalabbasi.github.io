this space is meant to be a tiny window into the life of someone who grew up at the nexus of two cultures.

my story is certainly not unique, but it is my own.

through recipes given by my mom, this space is hopefully a reminder that food is more then just a dictation of ingredients and steps.

it’s a collection of nostalgia, heritage, and tradition.

this space is a tribute to all of the kids who were too embarassed to bring their food to school.

it's a tribute to the kids who got excited seeing The Simpsons go to India.

it's a tribute to the kids who got excited hearing Chaiyya Chaiyya in Inside Man.

it's a tribute to the kids who got excited seeing Akshay Kumar dancing in Toronto.

and, most of all, this space is a tribute to the parents who gave up their homeland, language, and the taste of their mom’s cooking, so that their kids could have access to a world they could only dream about.

this one's not just for us.

it's also for them.

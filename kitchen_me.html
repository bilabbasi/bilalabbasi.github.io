this space

This space is meant to be a tiny window into the life of someone who grew up at the nexus of two cultures. My story is certainly not unique, but it is my own.

Told through recipes given by my mom, this platform is hopefully a reminder that food is more then just a dictation of ingredients and steps — it’s a collection of memories, heritage, and tradition.

This space is a tribute to the all of the kids who were made to feel like their food wasn’t normal. It's a tribute to the parents who had to improvise with whatever was missing from their pantry.

And, most of all, this space is meant to honour the parents who gave up their homeland, community, and the taste of their mom’s cooking, so that their kids could have access to a world they could only dream about.

This one's for the culture.
